# Example request

## User input

- **Target conference/journal:** PSCC 2026
- **General research question:** How can I perform reliable 15-minute unbalanced low-voltage distribution-system state estimation with sparse smart-meter voltage data, uncertain pseudo-measurements, and incomplete topology information?

## Scope interpretation

- Analyze the official PSCC 2026 proceedings.
- Screen direct state-estimation papers and enabling work on measurement placement, state forecasting, topology identification, robustness, bad-data handling, and probabilistic inference.
- Deep-read approximately 5–10 papers, prioritizing direct relevance and methodological diversity.

## Expected output spine

1. Overall assessment of the PSCC 2026 contribution landscape.
2. Ranked paper table with Core, Enabling, and Adjacent labels.
3. Per-paper analysis:
   - method and experiments;
   - relevance to the stated LV task;
   - transferable ideas;
   - author-stated and task-specific limitations;
   - one grounded research opportunity;
   - role in the project.
4. Cross-paper gaps, for example:
   - uncertainty is modeled but not demonstrably calibrated;
   - realistic LV measurement availability is incompletely represented;
   - topology and state uncertainty are handled separately;
   - sensor placement and estimator design are decoupled.
5. Prioritized opportunities, from a near-term covariance-aware sensor-placement study to a longer-term graph-temporal-physics estimator.
6. A dependency-aware sequence beginning with a defensible physics/UQ baseline and ending with real-system validation.

This example defines the shape only. The actual findings must come from reading the scoped papers.
