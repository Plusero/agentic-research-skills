Create one card per selected paper before drafting the final synthesis.

## Card sections

- [Bibliographic identity and evidence access](#bibliographic-identity-and-evidence-access)
- [Relevance screening](#relevance-screening)
- [Paper content](#paper-content)

- [Mapping to the target research question](#mapping-to-the-target-research-question)
- [Candidate opportunity signals](#candidate-opportunity-signals)
- [Unresolved evidence and verification](#unresolved-evidence-and-verification)

Complete only fields that apply to the paper and the target research question. Use
`Not applicable` when a field does not fit the study, `Not reported` when the
accessible text does not supply it, `Not accessible` when the necessary source is
unavailable, `Not assessed` when it still needs to be checked, and `Conflicting` when
accessible sources or versions disagree.

Keep paper-supported statements separate from analyst inference. Give every material
extraction a pinpoint location such as a page and section, figure, table, equation, or
appendix. Use `High`, `Medium`, or `Low` evidence confidence to describe how clearly
the accessible source supports an extraction—not whether the paper's claim is
universally true. For analyst inference, use the same scale to describe the strength
of the cited evidence chain and the number and importance of assumptions required;
do not use it as novelty or feasibility confidence. Add rows to repeatable tables as
needed; if none are supported, write `No supported items found.`
